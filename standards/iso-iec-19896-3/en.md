## 2. What it is about

This part describes the requirements on the knowledge and skills of the people
who carry out and review an evaluation under the ISO/IEC 15408 series, working
by ISO/IEC 18045.

The first point is the subject, and in this neighbourhood it is unusual. What
gets judged is a person. Not a product, not a module, not a management system,
but the person passing a verdict on one of those.

The second point is why. An evaluation result is a statement about a product, but
it comes into being through the work of people. Where the same procedure is
applied by two differently capable people, two different results come out, and
neither of them broke a rule. The comparability a certification promises rests on
the people and not on the procedure alone.

The third point is a distinction that collapses in everyday use. Knowledge can be
examined. Skill can only be observed in work done. A passed course evidences the
first and says nothing about the second, and a piece of evidence pulling both
into one sentence says less than it appears to.

The fourth point is the separation of the two roles. Whoever tests and whoever
reviews the testing do not need the same thing. Reviewing is an activity of its
own with a view of its own, and a body in which the same person does both has the
second reading in name only.

The fifth point is the boundary to the house itself. What a house owes for the
competence of its own people stands in ISO/IEC 27001, 7.2, and this document
changes nothing about it. The shape of the question is the same; the subject is a
different one.

What does not stand here is the wording, nor the areas of knowledge and the
skills this part lists, nor their number. Anyone needing that opens a licensed
copy.

## 3. Whom it serves, and whom it does not

Anyone commissioning an evaluation who wants to know what to ask a body.

Anyone who has to read and place an evaluation result somebody else paid for.

A body that hires and trains such people and has to evidence their competence.

Not the person settling the competence of their own people in the management
system. That is ISO/IEC 27001, 7.2, and the explanation of it stands in
[ISO/IEC 27003](../iso-iec-27003/en.md).

Not the person having a module tested. That is
[ISO/IEC 24759](../iso-iec-24759/en.md).

Not the person needing the testing of a single mechanism. That is
[ISO/IEC 18367](../iso-iec-18367/en.md).

## 5. What a practitioner does with it

Ask a testing body not about its accreditation but about its people. The
accreditation is a statement about the body; the work is done by individual
people, and the question is what competence is evidenced for them.

Then split that answer into the examined and the observed. A course with an exam
and supervised work on a real engagement are two different pieces of evidence.

Then look at whether testing and reviewing are done by different people. That one
question says more about the reliability of a result than the length of the
report.

Then carry the shape over into the house itself. Anyone settling competence in
their own management system can separate the same two halves, and most do not.

In operation what stays is currency. Competence about one edition of a standard is
competence about that edition. After an edition changes it is an assertion about
the previous state.

## 6. Where it stops against the neighbour

Against [ISO/IEC 27003](../iso-iec-27003/en.md): there the competence requirement
of the management system is explained. Here the subject is the competence of
those evaluating a product, and the two meet only in shape.

Against [ISO/IEC 24759](../iso-iec-24759/en.md): there stands what a module is
measured against. Here stands who may measure.

Against [ISO/IEC 18367](../iso-iec-18367/en.md) and
[ISO/IEC 20543](../iso-iec-20543/en.md): there stand methods of testing. Here
stands the condition on the person applying them.

Against the other parts of the ISO/IEC 19896 series: they deal with other groups
of people. No chapter for them sits in this tree.

Against the ISO/IEC 15408 series and
[ISO/IEC 18045](../iso-iec-18045/en.md): there stand the criteria and the
methodology. This part presupposes both and says what somebody applying them has
to be able to do. No chapter for the ISO/IEC 15408 series sits in this tree.

## 7. Before and after

Presupposed is that an evaluation is in question at all, so a product with a
security claim.

Presupposed is an idea of what the result is meant to carry in the house itself.
A report nobody reads needs no competence question.

What follows is the engagement itself, run as a supplier relationship, and the
testing of the object under [ISO/IEC 24759](../iso-iec-24759/en.md) where a
cryptographic module is at issue.

## 8. Walk-through: asking a testing body about competence

This walk-through follows the pattern in
[tutorials/en.md](../../tutorials/en.md). The example is invented.

Assume a house procuring a device that manages signing keys and being handed an
evaluation report for it. The question is: what does the report say about the
people who wrote it?

Step 1, look for the people in the report. In this example it carries the body,
its accreditation, a date and no person.

Step 2, put the question. In this example an enquiry goes to the body: what
competence is evidenced for the people who did this work, and how was it
evidenced.

Step 3, split the answer into two halves. In this example the answer names for
both people a course with an exam, and for one of them two supervised engagements
as well. The second half is missing for the other person.

Step 4, look at the separation of roles. In this example the person with the
supervised engagements is also the one who reviewed. That is the right way round
and is noted as such.

Step 5, match the edition. In this example the evidence refers to an edition that
has since been replaced. That is a finding and not an accusation.

Step 6, write the boundary. In this example what stays open is what the missing
observed work of the second person means for the result. That is one row in the
risk register. The template stands in
[templates/registers/risk-register/en.md](../../templates/registers/risk-register/en.md).

What comes out of it: a question put, an answer split, a role separation checked,
an edition matched and one written row. What does not come out of it: a verdict
about the body. This chapter passes none, and the walk-through only establishes
that the statements are on the table.

The assumptions of this example: a report handed over, a body that answers, two
people involved. Anyone getting no answer has the actual finding at step 2 and not
at step 6.

## 10. Does it need a presentation, and for whom

For two of the five audiences yes, for three no. The answer stands
language-neutral in `meta.yaml` beside this file, with a reason per audience.

Briefly: practitioners need the sentence that a result is worth as much as the
person who produced it, and audit needs the sentence that the shape of the
competence question is the same and only the subject differs. For management,
engineering and all staff a no stands with its reason in the same file.
