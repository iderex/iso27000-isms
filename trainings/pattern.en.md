# PATTERN, TITLE OF THE TRAINING

This is the pattern for the course material of a training. The rules for it
stand in [en.md](en.md). The German version of this pattern stands in
[pattern.de.md](pattern.de.md).

Anyone writing a training copies this file to `trainings/<topic>/en.md`,
replaces the capitals and leaves the order of the six sections standing.

## 1. What this training assumes

WHAT SOMEBODY HAS TO BE ABLE TO DO ALREADY, IN ONE OR TWO SENTENCES. WHERE IT
IS A STEP OF THE LEARNING PATH, IT IS LINKED.

Example: step 0 of the learning path is assumed, in
[learning-path/step-0/en.md](../learning-path/step-0/en.md), so the terms risk,
control and scope.

## 2. What this training leaves out

WHAT DOES NOT APPEAR, AND WHY NOT. THIS SECTION STANDS AT THE START AND NOT AT
THE END.

The wording is left out. This training reproduces no standard wording. Where it
matters the clause stands beside it, to be opened in a licensed copy.

## 3. The material

THE ACTUAL CONTENT, IN NUMBERED SECTIONS. EVERY SECTION ANSWERS A QUESTION THAT
COMES BACK IN THE QUESTION SET.

The material is built along actions rather than along clauses, unless the
training addresses an audience that actually works with the clauses.

References are by standard, clause and edition, such as ISO/IEC 27001:2022,
9.2. A control is addressed by its number, such as 5.15, and neither its title
nor its description is reproduced.

## 4. One worked place

AN EXAMPLE GOING FROM AN INVENTED STARTING CASE TO A RESULT. THE ASSUMPTIONS
STAND NAMED BESIDE IT, AND IT IS SAID THAT THEY ARE INVENTED.

A training without such a place tests recall at the end. Anyone who has worked
through it once can answer the question afterwards without remembering a
sentence.

## 5. Where the wording stands

WHICH CLAUSES AND WHICH EDITION ARE TO BE OPENED FOR THIS TOPIC, AND THE NOTE
THAT A LICENSED COPY IS NEEDED FOR THAT.

It also states against which sources the numbers named were checked and when.

## 6. What this training does not evidence

The record of the learning state arises in the importing system and not here. A
question set becomes a quiz there, the quiz produces attempts, scores and a
pass mark, and those stand in the importing system's course report. This
repository supplies material, questions and model answers and keeps no record
about any individual person.

This section is copied unchanged.
